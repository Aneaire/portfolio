# HOPLA.Online - Additional Insights & Strategic Notes

## The AI & Automation Division

HOPLA isn't just a traditional BPO. Their AI Workflow Project Manager role signals they're building out a dedicated **AI services delivery team**. This is significant:

- They work with **AI Engineers** and **Automation Specialists** — this is a real technical team, not just virtual assistants
- Projects involve **agentic systems**, LLMs, and automation platforms — cutting-edge AI work
- 2-week delivery cycles with client-facing work means this is a **managed services / consulting** model for AI

## What This Means for You

### Your Competitive Advantages

1. **Technical background** — most PM applicants won't have hands-on experience with n8n, APIs, and building AI-integrated apps. You do.
2. **Portfolio proof** — you can show actual projects, not just talk about concepts
3. **Builder mindset** — you understand what engineers go through because you are one. This makes you a better bridge between client and team.

### Potential Concerns to Prepare For

1. **"Are you more of a developer than a PM?"** — Frame your technical skills as an asset for the PM role, not a sign you'd rather be coding
2. **"Only 2–3 years experience?"** — Emphasize density of experience and ownership, not just time served
3. **Time tracker comfort** — They asked this in the application, which means some candidates push back. Be matter-of-fact about it.

## Industry Context

### AI Services Market in 2026
- Companies are racing to adopt AI workflows but lack internal expertise
- This creates demand for managed AI service providers like what HOPLA is building
- PMs who understand AI delivery are rare and valuable — most PMs are generalists

### HOPLA's Positioning
- They're not competing with McKinsey or Accenture — they're the **affordable, agile alternative** for companies that want AI automation without enterprise pricing
- The "30–70% lower cost" model means clients expect high value with fast delivery
- This means the PM role is high-impact: you're directly responsible for client satisfaction and project success

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Ask about **team size** — how many PMs, how many engineers, how many active client projects
- Ask about **client load** — managing 2 projects is very different from 8
- Ask about **overtime expectations** — "flexible to accommodate client time zones" can mean different things
- Ask about **career progression** — Glassdoor reviews mentioned limited promotion paths
