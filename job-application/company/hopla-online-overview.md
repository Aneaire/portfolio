## What They Do

HOPLA.Online is a distributed outsourcing company that connects work-from-home professionals with fast-growing, innovative companies worldwide. They call their model **"Outsourcing 2.0"** — combining the benefits of conventional outsourcing with modern remote-first technology and processes.

They provide companies with highly qualified home-based professionals across various roles, offering costs **30–70% lower** than traditional outsourcing providers.

## Services

- Content Moderation
- UX/UI Design
- Virtual Assistants
- Creative Design & Editing
- Data Entry
- Customer Support
- Content & Program Development
- Sales & Business Development
- **AI & Automation workflows** (the team you'd be joining)

## Founding Story

Founded by a group of entrepreneurs who previously worked with traditional outsourcing companies and freelance platforms. They identified that sourcing, hiring, and managing remote staff was unnecessarily hard, and built HOPLA to solve that with better infrastructure, technology, and remote processes.

### What Employees Like
- Flexibility of WFH
- Positive, friendly, and approachable environment
- Supportive culture with regular check-ins
- Team activities and group dynamics

### What Employees Flag
- Benefits package could be stronger
- Some concerns about internal issue resolution speed
- Limited promotion pathways mentioned by some

## Key Differentiators

1. **Fully remote** — no office, WFH is the default
2. **Global clients** — Europe, Asia, Australia, USA
3. **"WFH++"** — they go beyond basic remote flexibility with mentorship, learning paths, and stability
4. **Long-term roles** — they emphasize full-time positions, not gig work
