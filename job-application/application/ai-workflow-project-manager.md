## About the Role

Not a slow internal corporate role. You work directly on client projects, help turn messy requests into clear actions, and keep delivery moving with speed and structure. Ideal for someone with **2–3 years experience** in digital projects, operations, client delivery, or project coordination who is excited by AI.

## Daily Responsibilities

- Help manage delivery across multiple client projects in **2-week cycles**
- Turn client requests into clear tasks with **acceptance criteria** and next steps
- Keep priorities, owners, blockers, and statuses up to date in **Linear**
- Join client calls, capture decisions, and follow through on actions
- Work closely with **AI Engineers** and **Automation Specialists**
- Support sprint planning, refinement, reviews, and weekly client updates
- Make sure deliverables are testable, clear, and ready for release
- Spot risks early, ask good questions, prevent avoidable confusion/rework
- Help improve delivery, communication, and quality processes
- Learn how AI workflows, automations, and agentic systems are designed and delivered

- Strong organizational skills and high personal ownership
- Excellent spoken and written English

- Comfortable in fast-moving environments with changing priorities
- Turn vague requests into structured tasks and follow-ups

- Confident using project tools (Linear, Jira, Asana, Notion, or similar)
